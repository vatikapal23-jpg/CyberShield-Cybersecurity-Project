# Historical Cybersecurity Case Review: 2010–2025

## Key defensive themes
- Ransomware and disruption: WannaCry, Atlanta, Norsk Hydro, Colonial Pipeline, Costa Rica government systems, MGM Resorts, Change Healthcare.
- Supply-chain risk: NotPetya, SolarWinds, Kaseya VSA, 3CX, Trust Wallet browser-extension theft.
- Identity and credential risk: Twitter account takeover, Uber, Snowflake-related credential theft, OCC email compromise.
- Vulnerability management: Equifax, ProxyLogon, Log4Shell, MOVEit, SharePoint exploitation.
- Critical infrastructure: Stuxnet, Ukraine's power grid, Shamoon, Colonial Pipeline.

## Recommendations
1. Use MFA, preferably phishing-resistant for privileged/remote access.
2. Inventory internet-facing assets and software dependencies.
3. Patch critical exposed systems and verify remediation.
4. Segment IT/OT where feasible and tightly control remote access.
5. Keep offline or immutable backups and test restores.
6. Monitor privileged access, mailbox rules, bulk downloads, and unusual software updates.
7. Review third-party access and incident notification processes.
8. Preserve evidence and avoid premature attribution.
9. Verify each case against primary sources before citing it.

## Interpretation
Cases illustrate confidentiality (data theft), integrity (unauthorized/destructive changes), and availability (outages). These are qualitative observations from selected public cases, not statistical estimates. Selection and reporting are uneven, so the dataset cannot establish global year-to-year trends.
