# Basic Cyber Incident Response Playbook (Educational)

This checklist is generic and must be adapted to the incident, organisation, and current official instructions.

1. **Safety first:** address immediate physical danger and urgent financial harm.
2. **Pause the interaction:** do not pay, disclose secrets, approve authentication prompts, or follow suspicious instructions.
3. **Notify the appropriate party:** bank/payment provider for suspected financial fraud; organisational IT/security team for workplace devices/accounts; platform/provider for account abuse.
4. **Contain carefully:** isolate affected devices from networks when appropriate, especially suspected malware/ransomware, but avoid actions that destroy evidence or create safety risks.
5. **Preserve evidence:** retain original messages, email headers if available, transaction/reference numbers, dates/times, sender identifiers, screenshots, and relevant logs. Keep a simple evidence/action log.
6. **Secure access:** from a trusted device, reset exposed credentials, revoke active sessions, check recovery email/phone, enable MFA, and contact service providers.
7. **Recover:** use known-clean backups and verified software; validate system integrity before reconnecting.
8. **Report:** use official reporting channels for the jurisdiction. In India, start at https://cybercrime.gov.in/ and consult current official guidance; the 1930 helpline is commonly used for financial cyber fraud.
9. **Learn and improve:** document root cause, impact, controls that failed, corrective actions, owners, and deadlines.

Never guarantee that paying a ransom restores files or prevents disclosure. Do not independently investigate or confront suspected offenders.
